Pink slip questions, answered

Do I need a pink slip?

Most NSW vehicles over five years old need a pink slip (eSafety check) before registration renewal. If you're not sure whether yours is due, tell us your rego details when you call and we'll confirm.

How much does a mobile pink slip cost?

Prices start from $99 with no call-out fee on quoted work.

How long is a pink slip valid?

Your inspection result is electronically lodged with TfNSW and is generally valid for your registration renewal.

Can I get a same-day pink slip?

Yes. Same-day appointments are available across most suburbs, subject to availability.

What happens if my car fails?

We'll explain the issues clearly and guide you through the next steps before a reinspection.
